OPERATION BLACK BUCK - KEY FACTS

Total Distance

6,600 nautical miles round trip (12,200 km) - the longest bombing raid in history at that time, surpassing even the USAAF's Tokyo raids.

Mission Duration

15 hours 45 minutes flight time for the Vulcan, with over 105 hours of combined Victor tanker flight time supporting the mission.

Fuel Consumed

244,000 imperial gallons (1.1 million litres) of aviation fuel uplifted by all aircraft combined during the operation.

The Challenge

The Vulcan's combat radius was only 2,500 miles - far short of the 3,300 mile one-way distance from Ascension to Stanley.

Tanker Cascade

In an extraordinary logistical feat, fuel passed through up to 5 different tanker aircraft before finally reaching the Vulcan bomber.

Three Sections

Victor tankers were organized into Red, White, and Blue sections, plus five return tankers, performing an intricate aerial ballet in darkness.
