Sources & method

Royal Air Force — 40th Anniversary of Operation BLACK BUCK

Official overview: 11 tankers, the reserve Vulcan taking over, nearly 6,600 nm and about 16 hours; reports 14 air-to-air refuellings, seven involving the Vulcan.

RAF Air & Space Power Review — Black Buck to the Falklands

Detailed Black Buck 1 narrative: 730, 1,000, 1,650 and 2,340 nm brackets; broken Victor probe; last transfer 350 nm from Stanley; return rendezvous off Brazil.

RAF Historical Society Journal 30 — The Falklands Campaign

Planning doctrine and totals: 16 h 02 min Vulcan flight; 244,000 imperial gallons uplifted by the Victor waves; 7% received by the Vulcan and 20% transferred between Victors.

RAF Museum — Avro Vulcan, Part 4

Context on the restored refuelling system, the 21 × 1,000 lb load and the difficulty of probe-and-drogue contact from the Vulcan cockpit.

Fact: dates, distances, sequence, aircraft counts, timings and quoted totals come from the sources above. Inference / editorial model: generic tanker labels V1–V11, exact lateral aircraft positions, individual fuel gauges, return-flight curves and animation timing are illustrative. Sources disagree slightly on counting transfers (14 air-to-air refuellings in one RAF summary; 15 fuel transfers in its pilot narrative), so this experience depicts documented brackets rather than claiming an exact engineering ledger.
