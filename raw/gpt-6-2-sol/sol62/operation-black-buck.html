    How the relay worked

    The tankers needed tankers, too

    As the formation headed south, some Victors gave fuel to other Victors and turned for Ascension. The aircraft that received fuel could continue farther alongside the Vulcan. The RAF Museum records that fuel at the final outbound handoff had passed through five different tankers.

    The last outbound Victor, XL189, gave the Vulcan enough fuel to continue but needed an extra tanker to get home. A separate rendezvous off Brazil gave the Vulcan its last refuel on the way back. RAF Museum ↗
