Operation Black Buck 1

On the night of 30 April–1 May 1982, a single Avro Vulcan bomber struck the runway at Port Stanley, Falkland Islands — the longest-range bombing raid in history at the time (~6,800 nmi round trip, ~16 hours). Getting one bomber to target required a cascade of Handley Page Victor tankers refuelling each other and the Vulcan in a relay, dropping out one by one as their fuel was passed forward.

This simulation models that refuelling cascade: watch the fuel state of every aircraft, the refuelling brackets, and the famous crisis on the final outbound transfer.
