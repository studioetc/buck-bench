BLACK BUCK ONE

A single Avro Vulcan against the farthest target in RAF history. It could not get there and back — so eleven Victor tankers launched with it, refuelling the bomber and each other in a cascading relay down the South Atlantic, peeling away one by one until a lone tanker made the final top-off inside a thunderstorm — and then had to be rescued itself. Watch the fuel gauge. In this mission, fuel is the plot.

MISSION COMPLETE

XM607 is down after 15 hours 3 minutes and ~6,600 nautical miles — the longest bombing mission ever flown at that time. One crater in Stanley's runway, from one bomb in twenty-one. The Vulcan took on fuel eight times; four more Victors flew as the recovery wave — including one sent to refuel a tanker that had given away too much. Every aircraft came home.
