Reading this map

Documented facts include the aircraft counts, the four approximate bracket distances (800, 1,700, 2,600 and 3,000 nautical miles from Ascension), the fuel transfers, and the sequence of failures and recoveries. The exact aircraft tracks and event times were not published in the cited accounts. This replay places bracket markers along the shortest geographic route and interpolates aircraft motion between reported milestones. The clock between milestones is illustrative. Map symbols are enlarged and the refuelling aircraft are spread apart so the relay stays legible.

The cascading plan

At each bracket, donor tankers filled the aircraft going farther south, then turned for Ascension. By the final outbound transfer, one Victor—Bob Tuxford’s—was supporting Martin Withers’ Vulcan. The thunderstorm at Bracket 3 broke a tanker’s refuelling probe and put both the mission and Tuxford’s return at risk.
