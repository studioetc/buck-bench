Operation Black Buck 1 — 30 April / 1 May 1982

A single Vulcan bomber striking Port Stanley airfield from Ascension Island — a 3,886-nautical-mile leg each way — required eleven Victor K.2 tankers in a cascading aerial refuelling relay. Tankers fed tankers fed tankers fed the bomber, peeling off in sequence as their reserves drained. This is the choreography of that mission, compressed from sixteen hours into roughly ninety seconds.
