Sources and method

This is a reconstruction, not a flight log. The story, the order of events and the anchor figures come from published accounts. The fuel quantities, exact timings and the formation choreography are a model tuned to fit those anchors.

Documented and used as anchors

  • Eleven Victor K2 tankers and two Vulcans left Wideawake from 22:50Z at one-minute intervals. Red and White sections had four Victors each. Blue had three Victors and both Vulcans.
  • Primary Vulcan XM598 (Reeve) could not pressurise. White 4 (Milligan, XL163) had a jammed hose unit. Reserve XM607 (Withers) and reserve Victor XH669 (Biglands) took over.
  • Transfers at about 1 h 45 (730 nm), 2 h 30 (1,000 nm), 1,650 nm, and 5 h 30 (2,340 nm). The final transfer was about 350 nm north-east of Stanley and stopped with the Vulcan 6,000 lb short of full.
  • Biglands's probe broke in a storm near 40°S while taking fuel from Tuxford (XL189). The two swapped roles. Skelton turned back with a fuel leak. Brooks (White 3, XL192) passed fuel to Biglands.
  • Descent 250 nm out, pop-up 40 nm out, 21 × 1,000 lb bombs from 10,000 ft at about 30° to the runway at 04:38 local. “Superfuse” was sent at 07:46Z.
  • Recovery wave: four Victors at 05:20Z, a reserve at 05:30Z, two more about 07:30Z. Seymour met Tuxford over a thousand miles out. Neal refuelled XM607 off Brazil with a Nimrod assisting. The Vulcan arrived with under 8,000 lb.
  • Tuxford was airborne 14 h 05 min. XM607 landed at 14:52Z, 16 h 02 min after the first take-off. Victor K2 fuel load 123,000 lb. Vulcan about 74,000 lb, burning 16,250 lb/h when heavy.

Modelled or inferred

  • All fuel figures on screen. Burn rates rise with weight and were tuned so that the documented outcomes appear: tankers landing low, Tuxford unable to get home alone, the Vulcan short at the last transfer.
  • Who filled whom at brackets 1 to 3, beyond the names above. Red-to-White pairing by number is assumed. Todd as Red 1 and Skelton as White 1 are inferred from their roles.
  • The Vulcan's top-ups at bracket 3 and from White 3. Sources count seven or eight Vulcan contacts in all. The model shows eight, including the trial contact after the probe broke.
  • The second reserve Victor's turn-back point, the recovery-wave top-up point, the Nimrod's times, and Barrett's sortie. Accounts differ on which returning tanker Barrett met. The tanker sent to meet Skelton is not drawn.
  • The rendezvous position off Brazil, the route as a direct great circle, and all speeds. Sources give the take-off as 22:50Z or 23:00Z.
  • Recovery and rescue labels are descriptive. They are not historical callsigns.
