Operation Black Buck
1 May 1982
The Longest Bombing Raid in History
Vulcan bombers of No. 101 Squadron RAF launched from Ascension Island to attack the Argentine-held runway at Port Stanley — a round trip of nearly 8,000 miles across the South Atlantic. The mission required an extraordinarily complex aerial refuelling plan: eleven Victor K2 tankers refuelling each other and the lone Vulcan in a cascading relay, peeling off one by one until a single bomber pressed on alone to the target.
