Mission brief

One Avro Vulcan B.2 and eleven Handley Page Victor K.2 tankers launch from Wideawake Airfield, Ascension Island. Victors refuel Victors and the bomber in a cascading chain — the longest bombing raid in history at the time (~6,600 nm / 16 hours).

How the chain works

  1. Package climbs south from Ascension.
  2. Outer Victors top up inner Victors + Vulcan.
  3. Emptied tankers peel off and recover north.
  4. Remaining tankers become the next “givers”.
  5. Final tanker tops Vulcan alone for the bomb run.
  6. Return RV off Brazil restores the bomber home.
