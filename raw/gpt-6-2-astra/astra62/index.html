The map follows the first Black Buck mission. Aircraft are enlarged and separated into lanes. Each chapter runs for the same screen time. Curves, relative positions, group sizes after departure and transfer animations illustrate the story rather than measured flight data. The detail view enlarges the changing relationships between aircraft.

The RAF anniversary account includes Martin Withers’s recollections. The Vulcan to the Sky Trust identifies XL189 and describes the recovery tanker. The RAF100 Schools Project gives the elapsed flight time. Published totals for refuellings differ between accounts, so the replay groups transfers by chapter.
