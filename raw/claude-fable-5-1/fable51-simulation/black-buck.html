How this simulation was built

The sequence of events, callsigns, tail numbers, captains, Zulu times and the documented fuel figures come from published accounts of Black Buck 1. Positions, exact bracket latitudes, speeds and the minute-by-minute fuel states are modelled to be consistent with those facts — they are indicative, not archival.

Anchored on the record

  • Take-off stream from Wideawake at 2350Z, one-minute intervals; Reeve's XM598 11th off, Withers' XM607 last. XM598's DV-window seal failed; XM607 became the attack aircraft. Twenty minutes in, Victor XL163 returned with a hose fault and reserve XH669 (Biglands) replaced it.
  • Three sections: Red (4 Victors), White (4 Victors), Blue (3 Victors + 2 Vulcans). First transfers at T+1h45; Red and White paired up; Blue 1 (Wg Cdr Colin Seymour) refuelled the Vulcan twice and landed after 4h40m behind four returning Victors.
  • "In the 34 minutes between the first and second refuellings, Withers's Vulcan burned through 9,200 lb of fuel, at the rate of 16,250 lb per hour."
  • After the second bracket two more tankers turned back leaving three: Tuxford (XL189, White 2), Biglands (XH669, White 4) and the Vulcan. Brooks (XL192, White 3) had filled Biglands.
  • Thunderstorms near 40°S: Biglands' probe broke while taking fuel from Tuxford. Roles reversed; Biglands gave back what he could spare. Tuxford, who was to have turned home with 64,000 lb, continued south below his safe minimum and gave XM607 7,000 lb less than planned (RV fuel 7,000 lb instead of 14,000).
  • Low-level run-in at 300 ft, pop-up to 10,000 ft, 330 kt; H2S locked on Mount Usborne; 21 × 1,000 lb bombs; "Superfuse" at 0746Z. Bomb time shown as 0738Z (04:38 local per the Vulcan captain's account); some sources quote 0746Z.
  • Recovery wave: four Victors at 0520Z (one u/s at once, reserve at 0530Z), two more at ~0730Z — Seymour (XL511) to find Tuxford, Barrett to help a returning Victor with a fuel leak. A Nimrod helped bring XM607 and Neal's XH672 together off Brazil; the Vulcan arrived an hour late with under 8,000 lb across its 14 tanks.
  • Tuxford landed 14h05m after take-off (≈1355Z); Withers touched down 1452Z. Tuxford received the AFC, Withers the DFC.

Modelled / uncertain

  • Great-circle track Ascension–Stanley (3,380 nm); formation ground speed ≈445 kt; bracket positions derived from elapsed time. The real track and RV positions are approximate.
  • Victor fuel loads (110,000 lb at take-off, ~123,000 lb capacity) and burn rates are estimates chosen to reproduce the documented figures above. Treat all Victor gauges as indicative.
  • Tail numbers for Red 1/2/4, White 1 and the unused Blue reserve are not tied to callsigns in the sources; the eleven Victors were XH669, XH672, XL162, XL163, XL188, XL189, XL192, XL232, XL511, XL513 and XM717.
  • Accounts differ on where Seymour found Tuxford ("over a thousand miles" vs "600 miles" from Ascension) — the RV is placed ~1,250 nm out.
  • The reserve Blue Victor is labelled "Blue 5" by inference (Vulcans are believed to have been Blue 2 and Blue 4).
  • The chain diagram uses a non-linear time axis: refuelling brackets are stretched, long cruises compressed.
